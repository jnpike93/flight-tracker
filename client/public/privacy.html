Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 14, 2026 • Last updated: October 2026
Summary: AeroLog (flights.cuteanimegirls.moe) is a private flight logging and statistics visualization platform. We strictly respect your privacy. We do not sell your personal data or Google user data, and we do not use your information for advertising.

1. Information We Collect

AeroLog collects only the data necessary to provide personalized flight logging and mileage statistics:

  • Account Details: When you authenticate via Google OAuth, we receive your email address and profile name to verify access against our authorized allowlist and maintain your secure session.
  • Flight Logs: Flight details that you manually enter, import, or upload (flight number, airline, departure/destination airports, dates, seat assignment, aircraft type, and personal notes).
  • Google Calendar Events (Optional): If you choose to enable automated Calendar Sync, we read flight itinerary events from your primary Google Calendar strictly to extract flight numbers and airport codes for your logbook.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Authenticate and verify your identity against the system allowlist.
  • Calculate total miles flown, flight hours, visited airports, and country counts.
  • Render your personal 3D interactive flight globe and 2D route map.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

AeroLog's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your Google user data to any third party.
  • We do NOT share your Google user data with data brokers, ad networks, or marketing agencies.
  • We do NOT use Google user data to serve targeted advertisements.
  • We do NOT use Google user data to train AI or machine learning models.

4. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored in isolated SQLite databases with encryption in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Authentication sessions are secured via cryptographically signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT). We retain your data as long as your account remains active on the allowlist.

5. Data Deletion & User Rights

You can export your flight data to JSON anytime via the application navigation bar. You may also request complete and permanent deletion of your account and all associated flight records by contacting the administrator at miloverdrive@protonmail.com.
